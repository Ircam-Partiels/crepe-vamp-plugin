Models
------

The models embedded in the Resources/models folder of the plugin bundle are
the pre-trained models of CREPE (https://github.com/marl/crepe) by Jong Wook
Kim, Justin Salamon, Peter Li and Juan Pablo Bello, converted to the
TensorFlow Lite format (see resource/create-tflite-models.py in the source
repository of the plugin). They are distributed under the MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2018 Jong Wook Kim (crepe.txt).

- crepe-tiny, crepe-small, crepe-medium, crepe-large and crepe-full
  https://github.com/marl/crepe

J. W. Kim, J. Salamon, P. Li, J. P. Bello. "CREPE: A Convolutional
Representation for Pitch Estimation", Proceedings of the IEEE International
Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 2018.
